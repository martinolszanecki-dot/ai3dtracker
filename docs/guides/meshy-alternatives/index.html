Meshy alternatives (2026): cheaper, free and pro options

Affiliate disclosure: links marked as sponsored (currently Meshy) are affiliate links – we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Other links are plain official URLs. Affiliate status never changes our data. Data checked 2026-10-16/19 — always verify pricing on the vendor’s site.

Meshy is a strong all-rounder (text, image and multi-view input, texturing, 600+ animations, many export formats). Here is when another tool may fit better – and the full data to decide.

More credits for the same $20: Tripo

Tripo Pro: 3,000 credits (≈200 models, ≈$0.16 each by Tripo’s estimate) vs Meshy Pro’s 1,000 credits. Free tier is non-commercial.

Try Tripo →

Pay only for results you keep: Rodin (Hyper3D)

Rodin’s pay-by-result flow lets you preview before spending credits; Creator is $30/mo for about 60 models.

Try Rodin (Hyper3D) →

Unlimited generations: Sloyd

Sloyd Plus/Pro offer unlimited text/image-to-3D (fair use) from $12.50/mo billed yearly.

Try Sloyd →

Free & self-hostable: Hunyuan3D (Tencent)

Open weights plus a pay-as-you-go API from $15 per 1,000 credits.

Try Hunyuan3D (Tencent) →

3D printing focus: Hitem3D (Hi3D)

Split to Print, multi-color printing and send-to-printer features.

Try Hitem3D (Hi3D) →
