What a model costs in credits

Meshy 7.1 / Meshy 6: 20 credits untextured; 30 with 2K/4K texture; 35 with 8K texture (text- or image-to-3D); Ultra geometry +5 (7.1 only). Meshy 6 Lite: 5 untextured / 15 textured (2K only). Retexture 10–15. Meshy Edit: 20 credits per edit. (Meshy Help Center, Meshy API pricing)
