Tripo alternatives (2026)

Affiliate disclosure: links marked as sponsored (currently Meshy) are affiliate links – we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Other links are plain official URLs. Affiliate status never changes our data. Data checked 2026-10-16/19 — always verify pricing on the vendor’s site.

Tripo offers generous credits ($20 for 3,000) and STL/3MF export, but its free plan is non-commercial and Tripo retains rights to free outputs. Alternatives by need:

Commercial use on a free plan: Meshy

Meshy free outputs are CC BY 4.0 – usable commercially with attribution.

Try Meshy →

Preview before paying: Rodin (Hyper3D)

Rodin generates for free and charges when you confirm a result.

Try Rodin (Hyper3D) →

Print-first workflow: Hitem3D (Hi3D)

Hitem3D focuses on split-to-print and multi-color printing.

Try Hitem3D (Hi3D) →

Open weights: Hunyuan3D (Tencent)

Hunyuan3D can be self-hosted under Tencent’s community license.

Try Hunyuan3D (Tencent) →
