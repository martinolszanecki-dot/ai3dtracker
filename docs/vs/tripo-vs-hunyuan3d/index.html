Tripo vs Hunyuan3D (Tencent)

Short answer: pick Tripo if you want a polished subscription app with commercial rights on paid plans, print-ready STL/3MF export and DCC bridges. Pick Hunyuan3D if you want Tencent's hosted model with a daily free quota, pay-as-you-go API credits with no subscription, or open weights you can run yourself.

  • Models: Tripo's flagship is H3.1 (up to 2M faces) plus the P1.0 Smart Mesh model; Tencent hosts Hunyuan3D 3.1 (up to 8 input views), while the newest open-weight release is 2.1.
  • Price: Tripo Pro is $20/mo billed annually for 3,000 credits (≈200 models, ≈$0.16 each by Tripo's figure). Hunyuan3D's API sells credit packs from $15 per 1,000 credits; a textured Professional generation costs 25 credits (≈$0.34–0.375).
  • Free use: Tripo Free gives 200 credits/month but models are public and non-commercial. Tencent's global platform lists 20 free generations a day; the open-weight 2.1 license does not apply in the EU, UK or South Korea and needs a separate license above 1M monthly users.
  • Printing: both export STL; Tripo also exports 3MF and has an official print-prep guide.

Based on official pricing pages and docs checked 2026-10-19. We have not run a blind quality test of the two models.
