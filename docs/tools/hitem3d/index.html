Approx. cost per model: ≈$0.20 (Pro monthly, 10-credit v2.1 Fast) or ≈$0.60 for v3.0 Quality (30 credits); ≈$0.10 / ≈$0.30 billed annually; Max/Ultra ≈$0.10 per Fast model monthly – derived from Hitem3D's own figures (Hitem3D says 'as low as $0.05/model')

Free tier: Yes – 100 lifetime trial credits (up to 7 models); no downloads, no commercial use

Output & license

Inputs: Image, multi-view image

Export formats: FBX, OBJ, STL, GLB

Commercial license: Free: public models (CC BY 4.0), no commercial use. Pro/Max/Ultra: private models & commercial use.

Current model version: Hi3D v3.0 (Quality, Master) and v2.1 (Fast/Quality); Hi3D 1.0 (formerly Sparc3D) open-sourced Oct 23, 2026

Printability: Strong print focus: Split to Print, Multi-color Print, Smart Layout, Send to printer; slicer integrations listed: Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, Snapmaker, Creality Print, ElegooSlicer

Features

  • Texturing: Yes – texture generation incl. Stylized Texture (per site menu)
  • Rigging/animation: unknown
  • Retopology: unknown

Re-checked 2026-10-26 on the official pricing page: Max now 4,000 credits/month (was 2,400) at the same price; Ultra now $99.90/mo or $49.92/mo billed annually (was $129.90 / $64.95) with 10,000 credits (was 8,000); v3.0 Quality now 30 credits. The page runs a '50% off first month and first year' promotion and says the annual price is only available during it. The site now brands itself Hi3D (canonical www.hi3d.ai; www.hitem3d.ai serves the same site). Hi3D 1.0 open weights (Oct 23, 2026): a refinement model that turns a coarse mesh + reference image into high-res geometry; Hi3D Open License v1.0 allows commercial use, products over 200,000 MAU or USD 1M monthly revenue need a separate license, integrations must show 'Powered by Hi3D 1.0' (generated output files alone don't trigger this). Subscription credits refresh every 30 days and don't roll over; failed generations refunded. Integrates with ComfyUI and Blender. Hi3D's Oct 23 press release also announces Hi3D Agent and Hi3D MCP integration.
