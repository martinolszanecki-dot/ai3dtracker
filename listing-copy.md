## Long description (~500 characters)
AI 3D Tracker is a free, independent comparison database of AI text-to-3D and image-to-3D generators like Meshy, Tripo, Rodin, Hunyuan3D and Sloyd. For each tool it lists the paid starting price, estimated cost per model, the free tier, export formats (GLB, FBX, OBJ, STL, USDZ…), commercial-use license terms and 3D-printability. Data comes from official pricing pages, and anything unknown is clearly marked. A free weekly newsletter covers price and license changes.
(~495 chars)

Hi! I built AI 3D Tracker because comparing AI 3D generators was painful. Every tool prices in credits, the free-tier licenses differ (CC BY, non-commercial, public-only), and it's hard to tell which ones export STL or 3MF for printing. So I gathered the official pricing and terms for 15 tools into one table, with cost per model where it can be worked out, and marked anything I couldn't verify. It's free, and there's a weekly newsletter for price and license changes. I'd love to hear which tools or columns you'd like added.
